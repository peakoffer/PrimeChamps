# Arm B rubric (success profile)
Question: Would OnlyFans' sports sponsorship team approve a paid sponsorship
pitch for this athlete at the time of the cutoff?

What the sponsor historically values (weigh these; none alone is decisive):
- Must be 21+ (evidence of under-21 at the cutoff means reject). Unverified age
  is NOT a reason to reject here; judge fit on the merits.
- Active, current competitor in a sport with visible branding inventory the
  sponsor can use: fight kit/shorts/robes, race suit or vehicle livery, board,
  bike, boat, event appearances. Niche, action, water, motor and combat sports
  fit well. Strict league or promotion kit exclusivity (e.g. uniform rules that
  limit personal sponsor logos) reduces usable inventory.
- A meaningful personal audience for the athlete's tier and genuine creator
  behavior (training, behind-the-scenes, personality-led posts).
- Realistic price tier: accessible, mid-tier and rising athletes are favored;
  global stars whose fees exceed comparable deals are often declined.
- Existing, active OnlyFans creator activity is a strong positive; an existing
  but inactive or abandoned OnlyFans profile is a strong negative (the sponsor
  prefers to see organic platform use first).
- Current availability: a known long injury layoff, season already too far
  advanced, or no upcoming events at the cutoff is a negative.
- Renewals of an existing sponsored relationship are strongly positive.
